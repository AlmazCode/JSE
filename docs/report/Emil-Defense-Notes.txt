JSE — Emil's Bridge and Decorator defense notes

Your pattern responsibilities are Bridge and Decorator. The implementation is
integrated with the team's factories, AI and observers; module responsibility
is distinct from exclusive authorship of every integration edit.

Bridge
  Abstraction: jse.render.Graphic.
  Refined abstractions: RectangleGraphic and SpriteGraphic.
  Implementor: Renderer.
  Concrete implementors: FilledRenderer and WireframeRenderer.

Explain that a graphic delegates drawing to the current renderer. EntityGraphics
passes the frame's Renderer to each entity's graphic. Press F1 while paused:
the visible style changes, while World, IDs, positions and health are retained.
Both production styles use Java2D. A renderer does not retain Graphics2D after
endFrame; resources are preloaded, so drawing performs no resource IO.

Decorator
  Component: Graphic.
  Base decorator: GraphicDecorator, retaining a wrapped Graphic.
  Concrete decorators: OutlineDecorator and HealthBarDecorator.

The decorator first draws its wrapped component and then its own layer. Renderer
replacement forwards through the entire chain. HealthBarDecorator reads actual
Player HealthView values and clamps current/max; max must be positive. Outline
and health layers do not change collision bounds.

Press F2 and F3 separately and together. Explain why each change rebuilds from
baseGraphic rather than wrapping the already decorated graphic. This prevents
unbounded chain growth. GraphicLayers fixes the composition order, and
EntityGraphics restores retired objects to their bases and drops references.

Useful answers
  Why Bridge? New graphic kinds and renderer styles vary through separate
  abstractions. F1 demonstrates replacement without rebuilding game entities.

  Why Decorator? Optional appearance layers can be combined without one subclass
  for every possible outline/health combination.

  How do the two patterns interact? A decorated Graphic is still a Graphic; the
  Bridge implementor replacement propagates through every wrapped component.

  Where are the tests? RendererImageTest and EntityGraphicsTest assert real
  pixels after renderer replacement. GraphicDecoratorTest checks live health,
  independent layers and repeated toggles. Lifecycle tests exercise reset,
  theme and menu transitions.

  What remains a limitation? Windows/macOS and all native keyboard delivery are
  not verified. The event bus is synchronous with deferred nested events; it is
  not the stronger future next-tick batching protocol.
